COHORT NOTE
-----------
The Yin et al. 2024 paper restricted its bulk RNA-seq + scRNA-seq analyses to
FEMALES at month 8 postinfection (paper quote: "We limited these studies to
females because individuals with high levels of OR7D2 or ALAS2 were mostly
female"). To make a like-for-like comparison, the script defaults to a
female-only subset of our cohort, with the closest available timepoint being
6_month. Outputs land in `results_female/` so the original full-cohort run
under `results/` is preserved.

Our female cohort at 6_month (closest to paper's 8mo): 4 LC vs 4 Recovered
(subjects 110, 127, 147, 185 vs 129, 144, 151, 173).

QUICK START (paper-aligned, female-only, default)
-------------------------------------------------
  sbatch /users/hjiang/GenoDistance/long_covid/article/code/submit.sh

Each results_<cohort>/ folder contains figures/, tables/, summary/.

================================================================================
DE METHOD BY COHORT
================================================================================

  --cohort=all     uses pre-computed limma/edgeR DE under
                   .../step1/step1_cross_group/LC_vs_Recovered/

  --cohort=female  recomputes DE in-script: per-gene t-test on log pseudobulk
  --cohort=male    expression for cohort-restricted samples; logFC =
                   mean(LC) - mean(Recovered); FDR via Benjamini-Hochberg.
                   With ~4 vs 4 samples this is underpowered for genome-wide
                   FDR<0.05 hits but gives valid effect-size + direction
                   estimates for the targeted paper-gene comparisons.

================================================================================
PAPER CLAIMS BEING TESTED
================================================================================

  A. Cell type proportions differ (LC vs Recovered)
  B. Bulk RNA-seq: ALAS2, OR7D2 UP in LC (paper Fig. 5a; female-only cohort)
  C. CD4+ T cells: CXCR4, CXCR5, CCR4, CCR6 UP in LC (tissue-homing)
  D. CD8+ T cells: PDCD1, CTLA4, TIGIT, LAG3 UP in LC (exhaustion)
  E. CD8+ T cells: GZMA, GZMB, PRF1 UP; GZMK DOWN in LC (cytotoxic)
  F. Monocytes: ALAS2, IFIT2, IFIT3, ISG15 UP in LC (inflammatory)
