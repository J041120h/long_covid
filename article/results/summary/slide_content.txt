KEY METHODOLOGICAL NOTE:
-------------------------------------------------------
The paper's main analyses use CyTOF (protein) on antigen-specific T cells.
All CyTOF markers are protein-coding genes with a 1-to-1 gene-protein mapping:
  CXCR4 protein = CXCR4 gene | PD-1 protein = PDCD1 gene | etc.
Checking the same genes at mRNA level in our scRNA-seq is a valid approach
("transcriptomic validation of CyTOF findings") — mRNA and protein levels
correlate reasonably well for surface immune receptors.

The real caveat is the CELL POPULATION, not mRNA vs protein:
  Paper: ~1-5% of T cells (SARS-CoV-2-specific, peptide-stimulated + sorted)
  Ours:  100% of T cells (all T cells, no antigen selection)
→ Any signal enriched in the 1-5% antigen-specific cells will be diluted
  ~20-100x when averaged over all T cells in our pseudobulk data.

We split genes into two tiers based on what cell population the paper used:
  Tier 1 (green):  paper's scRNA-seq results using all PBMCs (same as us)
  Tier 2 (orange): paper's CyTOF on antigen-specific T cells (dilution expected)

SLIDE 3 — Comparability of Our Data
================================================================================
TITLE:  What We Can and Cannot Compare

BULLET POINTS:
  All CyTOF markers are protein-coding genes — gene name = protein name.
  Checking mRNA levels for CyTOF protein findings is valid science.
  The real question is cell population, not mRNA vs protein:

  Paper finding                     | Cell population       | Signal dilution?
  ----------------------------------|-----------------------|------------------
  ALAS2/OR7D2 (scRNA-seq, all PBMC) | All PBMCs (like us)  | None ✅
  Cell type proportions             | All PBMCs (like us)  | None ✅
  CXCR4/CCR6 mRNA (scRNA-seq valid.)| All PBMCs (like us)  | None ✅
  CXCR4/CXCR5 protein (CyTOF)      | Antigen-specific only | ~20-100x diluted ⚠️
  PD-1/CTLA4/TIGIT protein (CyTOF) | Antigen-specific only | ~20-100x diluted ⚠️

  Main caveat:
  • SARS-CoV-2-specific T cells = ~1-5% of all T cells
  • Their elevated CXCR4/PD-1 signal is averaged with ~95% of non-specific cells
  • Result: small effect size, hard to detect without antigen enrichment

SLIDE 4 — Cell Type Proportions (✅ Directly Comparable)
================================================================================
TITLE:  Cell Type Proportions: Significant Differences at 6 Months

BULLET POINTS:
  Directly comparable to paper (Extended Data Fig. 1 cluster distribution)
  Test: Wilcoxon rank-sum test on per-sample proportions (LC vs Recovered)

  Statistically significant (p < 0.05):
  At 6 months:
  • CD14+ Monocyte ↑ in LC   (LC=23.6%  vs Rec=14.5%,  p = 0.011) ★
  • Memory CD4+ T  ↓ in LC   (LC=12.9%  vs Rec=17.4%,  p = 0.028) ★
  At 1 month:
  • CD16+ Monocyte ↓ in LC   (LC=1.3%   vs Rec=2.8%,   p = 0.024) ★

  Trending (p < 0.10):
  • Lymphocyte ↓ in LC at 6M  (LC=17.1% vs Rec=23.9%,  p = 0.061)
  • Naive CD8+ T ↓ in LC at 6M (LC=3.8% vs Rec=5.7%,   p = 0.077)

  Interpretation:
  • CD14+ Monocyte expansion at 6M suggests persistent monocyte activation
    → Consistent with paper's monocyte inflammatory findings (Fig. 1)
  • Memory CD4+ T depletion at 6M → consistent with T cell dysregulation claim
  • CD16+ Monocyte depletion at 1M → early monocyte subset redistribution

SLIDE 5 — ALAS2 and OR7D2 (✅ Primary scRNA-seq Hit, Does Not Replicate)
================================================================================
TITLE:  ALAS2 and OR7D2: The Paper's Only scRNA-seq DE Genes — Not Replicated

BULLET POINTS:
  These are the ONLY two genes the paper found significantly DE by scRNA-seq
  (Extended Data Fig. 9, volcano plots of LC vs Recovered clusters)

  What the paper found:
  • ALAS2 (heme synthesis enzyme): UP in LC across multiple clusters
  • OR7D2 (odorant receptor): UP in LC across multiple clusters

  What we find in our data (20 cell-type × timepoint tests for ALAS2):
  • DOWN in LC: 13 out of 20 tests (65%) — opposite to paper
  • UP in LC:    7 out of 20 tests (35%)
  • Example: B Cell 1M logFC=-0.527; Lymphocyte 1M logFC=-1.150 (both DOWN)
  • No tests reach FDR < 0.05 (total: 311,088 tests, 0 significant genome-wide)

  Why inconsistent?
  • Paper and our study both used all-PBMC scRNA-seq — cell population matches
  • Most likely: small sample size (14 LC, 20 Rec) → underpowered to detect
    the modest effect sizes the paper reported
  • Cell type annotation differences may also play a role

SLIDE 6 — Tissue-Homing Receptors in CD4+ T (⚠️ mRNA proxy for CyTOF)
================================================================================
TITLE:  CD4+ T Cell Tissue-Homing Receptors: mRNA-Level Signal (Mixed)

BULLET POINTS:
  Context:
  • Paper (Fig. 2) measured CXCR4/CXCR5/CCR6 as PROTEINS by CyTOF
    on SARS-CoV-2 peptide-stimulated, antigen-specific CD4+ T cells
  • Paper found these markers UP in LC — indicating altered tissue homing

  What we find (mRNA in ALL Memory + Naive CD4+ T cells, mean logFC):
  Consistent with paper direction:
  • CCR4:   mean logFC = +0.113  ↑ in LC (weakly consistent)
  • CD40LG: mean logFC = +0.240  ↑ in LC (weakly consistent)

  Inconsistent with paper direction:
  • CXCR4:  mean logFC = −0.065  ↓ in LC  (paper found UP)
  • CXCR5:  mean logFC = −0.158  ↓ in LC  (paper found UP)
  • CCR6:   mean logFC = −0.336  ↓ in LC  (paper found UP)
  • ITGB1:  mean logFC = −0.025  ↓ in LC  (paper found UP)

  Why the inconsistency?
  • Paper analyzed SARS-CoV-2-specific T cells (~1-5% of all T cells)
  • We analyze 100% of CD4+ T cells — signal diluted by the non-reactive majority
  • 3 of 6 markers DOWN (opposite direction) — results are not interpretable
    as supporting the paper's CyTOF findings

SLIDE 7 — CD8+ T Exhaustion (⚠️ mRNA proxy for CyTOF)
================================================================================
TITLE:  CD8+ T Exhaustion Markers: GZMK Consistently Down in LC

BULLET POINTS:
  Context:
  • Paper (Fig. 3) found PD-1+CTLA4+ CD8+ T cells elevated in LC
  • Measured as PROTEINS by CyTOF on antigen-specific CD8+ T cells

  What we find in mRNA across all CD8+ T cells (mean logFC across timepoints):
  Consistent with paper direction:
  • GZMK:  mean logFC = −0.874  ↓ in LC ★ STRONGEST SIGNAL
            GZMK = early effector marker; its loss indicates exhausted/
            terminally differentiated cells replacing less-differentiated ones
            → consistent with exhaustion enrichment the paper described
  • GZMB:  mean logFC = +0.079  ↑ in LC (weakly consistent; paper expects UP)
  • TIGIT:  mean logFC = +0.025  ↑ in LC (weakly consistent; paper expects UP)

  Inconsistent with paper direction:
  • PDCD1 (PD-1): mean logFC = −0.166  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)
  • CTLA4:        mean logFC = −0.272  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)
  • LAG3:         mean logFC = −0.409  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)
  • HAVCR2 (TIM3):mean logFC = −0.159  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)
  • GZMA:         mean logFC = −0.260  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)
  • PRF1:         mean logFC = −0.069  ↓ in LC  (paper expects UP)

  Interpretation:
  • GZMK down-regulation is the most reliable and biologically coherent signal
  • Classical exhaustion markers (PD-1, CTLA4, LAG3) are not detectable
    without antigen-specific T cell enrichment

SLIDE 10 — Conclusions
================================================================================
TITLE:  Conclusions

BULLET POINTS:
  What we CAN confirm (statistically significant, p < 0.05):
  ✓ CD14+ Monocyte ↑ in LC at 6M  (LC=23.6% vs Rec=14.5%, p=0.011)
    → Supports paper's monocyte inflammatory finding (Fig. 1)
  ✓ Memory CD4+ T ↓ in LC at 6M   (LC=12.9% vs Rec=17.4%, p=0.028)
    → Supports paper's T cell dysregulation claim
  ✓ CD16+ Monocyte ↓ in LC at 1M  (LC=1.3% vs Rec=2.8%, p=0.024)
    → Early monocyte subset redistribution

  What we partially support (consistent direction, not significant):
  ~ GZMK ↓ in LC (mean logFC=−0.874 in CD8+ T) — biologically coherent
    mRNA signal consistent with less-differentiated effector T cell loss

  What we CANNOT confirm:
  ✗ ALAS2/OR7D2 scRNA-seq hits — our data shows opposite direction (65% DOWN)
  ✗ PDCD1/CTLA4/TIGIT exhaustion proteins — all inconsistent (dilution effect)
  ✗ CXCR4/CXCR5/CCR6 tissue-homing proteins — inconsistent (dilution effect)

  Why the mismatch?
  • For T cell genes: paper isolated SARS-CoV-2-specific T cells (1-5% of total)
    → Our pseudobulk averages over ALL T cells → signal diluted ~20-100x
  • For all genes: 14 LC vs 20 Rec is underpowered for pseudobulk DE
    → 0 FDR-significant genes genome-wide

  To strengthen replication:
  → Need antigen-specific T cell isolation, OR larger N (≥30 per group)
  → Most replicable in our data: cell proportion and monocyte findings
